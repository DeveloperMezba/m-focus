# M Focus

**Stay Focused. Stay Disciplined. Get More Done.**

M Focus is a personal productivity and focus-tracking application designed to help you minimize distractions, manage your focus sessions, and build consistent productivity habits.

With a clean interface, customizable focus sessions, and useful progress tracking, M Focus helps you take control of your time and stay committed to your daily goals.

## ✨ Features

- **Focus Timer:** Set customizable focus durations using an intuitive analog-style timer.
- **Quick Presets:** Start sessions quickly with predefined time options.
- **App Management:** Manage allowed applications during focus sessions.
- **Break Scheduling:** Organize your focus sessions with scheduled breaks.
- **Session Protection:** Monitor protection status and required permissions.
- **Daily Goals:** Set and track your daily focus targets.
- **Focus Streaks:** Build consistency and maintain your productivity streak.
- **Progress Reports:** Review your focus activity and productivity history.
- **Haptic & Sound Feedback:** Get interactive feedback while using the app.
- **Modern Interface:** Enjoy a clean, compact, card-based user interface.
- **Multiple Themes:** Choose between Light, Dark, and AMOLED Black themes.

## 📊 Productivity Tracking

M Focus is designed to help you understand your productivity through:

- Daily focus progress
- Focus session summaries
- Daily goals and streak tracking
- Activity reports

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

M Focus focuses on simplicity, clarity, and usability.

The interface is designed around:
- Minimal and clean layouts
- Rounded cards and smooth visual elements
- Clear progress indicators
- Easy-to-access controls
- A distraction-free experience

## 🚀 Project Goals

The goal of M Focus is to make focused work easier and more consistent by combining time management, distraction control, and productivity tracking in one application.
